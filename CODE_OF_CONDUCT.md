# Code of Conduct

SyncWatcher contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome different experience levels and ask clarifying questions in good faith.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and accept corrections professionally.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Stop behavior that a maintainer identifies as disruptive or unsafe.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [support@studiojin.dev](mailto:support@studiojin.dev). Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close conversations, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when reasonably necessary to protect the community. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing SyncWatcher or StudioJin.
